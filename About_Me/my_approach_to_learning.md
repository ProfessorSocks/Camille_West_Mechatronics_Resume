# My Approach to Learning

## Curiosity and Engineering

I am a curious and friendly engineering student who genuinely loves learning and building relationships with people. I find engineering fun, and I enjoy challenging myself with new concepts. My interests extend across mechanical, electrical, software, systems, and civil engineering.

I want to understand why something works, not just how to make it work.

## How I Learn

I enjoy exploring the mathematics, physics, and science behind my projects. I like using logic, calculations, and simulations to support my understanding.

I use AI-assisted learning, interactive tools, visual media, and independent projects to explore engineering concepts. Outside of university, I develop my own learning plans and study topics beyond my degree requirements.

I enjoy connecting theoretical knowledge to practical applications and understanding how different engineering disciplines work together.

## Collaboration and Growth

I value learning from experienced engineers and students from different disciplines. I enjoy exchanging ideas, building professional relationships, sharing knowledge, and receiving constructive feedback.

When I make a mistake, I want to understand why I was wrong rather than simply memorize the correct answer. I try to question my assumptions, investigate mistakes, and revise my understanding based on evidence.

Ultimately, I want to become an engineer who can think independently, solve unfamiliar problems, and use engineering to improve people's lives.
